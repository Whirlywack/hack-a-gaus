## Data-quality problems seen

- **No supplier yet (16 contracts, 7%).** All were signed 2026-09-04 to 2026-10-16 and have no Salis rows at all, buyer row included. This looks like the Salis loading lag; their value is EUR 874,298.85 in total, including the largest recent one (Šešuvies g., EUR 388,999.27, dok_id 2008241492).
- **Hashed suppliers (8 contracts).** `salies_asmens_rusis` = "Nežinomas", with code and name as 15-character hex strings. Code `9f88cc611035da6` (name `4aaf9a206f1dfe3`) holds 7 contracts, mostly technical supervision of road works; `b2fbe6bfcc56003` holds 1. They can be grouped but not named. No supplier is marked as a private individual.
- **End dates.** None are missing and none fall before the signing date. But for 7 imported small purchases (`dok_sut_tipas=8`, the footpath paving jobs from 2025–2026) the end date equals the signing date, so it carries no information.
- **Zero values.** None. The smallest is EUR 377.52.
- **Duplicate rows.** dok_id 2005591723 and 2005796331 share the contract number G1-127, signing date 2022-07-13, value EUR 6,475.68 and supplier (UAB "Uliksas"), and their titles differ by one space ("Pokonstrukcinio" vs "Po konstrukcinio"). Only the end dates differ (2022-08-16 vs 2022-07-29). This looks like one contract entered twice. Both are kept in the JSON.
- **Contract numbers reused within a year.** G1-73 in 2023 (2006251966 road marking, 2006270204 design work); G1-259 in 2026 (2008259414 and 2008262828: same date 2026-09-30 and the same EUR 33,000, but different titles and contract types); G1-60 in 2025 (2007646649 and 2008035254). Because of these, an amendment that cites only "G1-73" could not be linked by number. It was linked by its title.
- **A suspicious signing date.** dok_id 2008035254 (gravel-road grading, lot 1) says it was signed 2025-03-20, but its dok_id is in the 2026 range and its sibling lots 2–7 (2008028392) were signed 2026-03-17. The date may be a year off. It is kept as published.
- **Names spelt several ways.** 158257064 appears as `Uždaroji akcinė bendrovė 'Jurmelsta'` and `Uždaroji akcinė bendrovė "Jurmelsta"`. 301506046 appears as `"ELEKTRUM LIETUVA", UAB` and `"Elektrum Lietuva", UAB`. 305582970 appears as `MB Keliukas` and `Keliukas, UAB`, so even the legal form differs. The buyer is spelt in mixed case and all caps.
- **Amendments that do not read as amendments.** Five `dok_sut_tipas=5` rows have plain contract titles. Two are road works: 2006196612 "Privažiavimo kelio prie Statybininkų g. ... statybos darbai" (EUR 1,777,737) and 2007756174 "Vandens pralaidos įrengimo Raudonės sen. kelyje Nr. r21" (EUR 22,022). The others are 2005820764, 2007477398 and 2007906281. All five are left out of the list under the type-5 rule.
- **Amendments repeat the full value.** For example, contract G1-5 (2007951195, EUR 297,390.17) has three amendments showing EUR 297,390.17, 297,390.17 and 280,153.88. Summing amendments would double-count.
- **Actual value ×100.** In the 51 rows fetched with every field, `dok_fakt_sut_ivyk_verte` is 100× the contract value twice: 2008208733 (12,100 vs 1,210,000) and 2008271473 (9,447.35 vs 944,735). The other rows were fetched without that field.
- **Multi-code CPV.** 21 of 230 contracts carry 2 CPV codes separated by ";". `startswith` matches only the first code. Here the CPV test was applied locally to every code.

## What the filter catches badly

- **Misses.** Street rebuilds filed under CPV 45222000 and titled "... g." rather than "gatvė" do not match. Examples seen: 2008031109 "Pasnietalio g., ... kapitalinis remontas" (EUR 377,606.96) and 2008043563 "Logupio g., ... kapitalinis remontas" (EUR 244,365.54). They are not in the list.
- **False hits.** "keli" matches "kelionių" (travel), "kelių transporto" (bus tickets) and "keliams barstyti" (road salt). "dang" matches "padangų" (tyres) and "grindų dangos" (flooring). "tako" and "takų" match "latakų" (gutters). "stotel" matches EV-charging stations. "aikštel" matches playground equipment and cemetery upkeep.

## Coverage and route (how the list was built)

1. The buyer has 9,871 buyer rows in Salis, 5,349 of them with dok_id >= 2004500000. Paging them all, then joining them to Sutartis, would take about 55 requests, so that route was not used.
2. `Sutartis.user_id` = 188713933 returns only imported small purchases (147 road-filter rows, all `dok_sut_tipas=8`, all signed 2025-01-31 or later). Only the 7 with CPV 4523 are kept. The other 140 matched on a title stem only: bus and transport tickets, road salt, road and street signs, tyre repair, travel, motorway tolls, flooring and paving-slab goods.
3. The tendered contracts (dok_id 20xx) have `user_id` = null. Jurbarkas numbers them "G1-…". A nationwide query for road-filter contracts with a number starting "G1-" returned 251 rows. Every one with a Salis row has this buyer as its buyer: 200 of the first 200, and 33 of the last 51. The other 18 have no Salis rows yet. A second query for numbers like "CPO…/G1-…" added 6 rows, all verified.
4. **Completeness check.** In one sample band of 200 buyer rows (Sept–Nov 2023), the road filter matched 4 contracts. The G1 route had caught 1 of them. The CPO/G1 query then caught 1 more, a design-review service. The other 2 were oral small purchases (numbered "Žodinė sutartis": a tyre change and a gutter-cleaning kit) that match only on title stems. **Contracts whose number lacks "G1-" are therefore not covered.** That mostly affects oral or invoice-numbered small purchases from 2022–2024.
5. In total about 32 API requests were made, counting route probes (one jsonl request was refused by the browser and repeated as json). Every URL is listed in `jurbarkas.json` under `query_urls`; long dok_id OR-lists are abbreviated there.
