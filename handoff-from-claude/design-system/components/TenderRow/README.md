# TenderRow

A tender in a list: days left as the numeral on the left, then the title, the buyer and the deadline.

- **Use:** Every list of open tenders. Tags sit at the right of the deadline; a new tender gets a strip of two cell buttons under it.
- **Provide:** The tender (days left, title exactly as the buyer wrote it, buyer, deadline), optional tags, optional actions.
- **Do not:** Never rewrite or shorten a title; it clamps to two lines by itself. The numeral is `urgent` only at 7 days or fewer.
