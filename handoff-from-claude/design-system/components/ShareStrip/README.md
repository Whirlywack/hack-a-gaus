# ShareStrip

One bar cut into real shares, with a legend of three figures under it.

- **Use:** How concentrated something is: the leader or the five largest in `ink`, the rest in `mark`, the unpublished part hatched.
- **Provide:** Up to three parts, each with a number, a percentage and two or three words.
- **Do not:** Segments are drawn to scale. Never more than three. No accent.
