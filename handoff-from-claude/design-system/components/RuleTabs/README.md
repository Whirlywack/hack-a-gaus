# RuleTabs

A choice as a row of options, each under a 2px rule; the chosen one in the accent. With numbers and filled rules it shows a stage.

- **Use:** Filter a list (line of work), switch the trade, or show the stage of a followed tender: Stebiu, Rengiu, Pateikiau.
- **Provide:** The options, the chosen index; for a stage, numbered and with progress.
- **Do not:** Not more than three or four options. Do not use pills or a sliding thumb.
